<objective>
Strip ListenableServiceMixin and ReactiveValue<bool> from ConnectivityService and SyncService. Add bridge @riverpod providers for AppDatabase, ApiClient, ConnectivityService (as StreamProvider<bool>), and SyncService — all wrapping the existing GetIt singletons to prevent dual-instantiation.

Purpose: This is the most critical task in Phase 1. Three screens depend on the isSyncing signal; if ListenableServiceMixin is removed without a broadcast replacement in place, those screens silently lose sync status indicators. Bridge providers enforce the single-instance constraint while both DI systems coexist.

Output: Tiers 1 and 2 of the provider graph exist. appDatabaseProvider, apiClientProvider, connectivityServiceProvider (StreamProvider<bool>), syncServiceProvider — all resolvable from ProviderScope.
</objective>
